Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about working with Nova Titan Systems

What types of projects does Nova Titan bid on?

Nova Titan Systems actively bids Division 27/28 low-voltage subcontracting projects for commercial construction and government facilities. This includes structured cabling, access control systems, security camera infrastructure, Wi-Fi, AV/PA systems, communications systems, and Division 28 fire alarm rough-in coordination (regulated FA work by licensed TX partners at award).

What is your service area?

Our operating base is West Texas. Houston, DFW, and additional markets are handled project-by-project based on scope, location, and field-resource availability.

How quickly can you respond to a quote request?

We aim to acknowledge quote requests within one business day. Urgent requests are evaluated based on location, scope, and available field coverage. Proposal and scheduling timing depends on project requirements and resource availability.

Are you registered for government work?

Yes. Nova Titan Systems is SAM.gov registered with UEI CZ8BLZMSKZ66 and CAGE Code 170W6. We are a small business eligible for micro-purchase and simplified acquisition procurements.
