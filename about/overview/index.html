Our Mission

Nova Titan Systems LLC is a Lubbock-based Division 27/28 commercial low-voltage subcontractor supporting general contractors, commercial facility owners, public-sector procurement, and field-service partners across West Texas, Houston, and DFW.

Our core work includes structured cabling, low-voltage infrastructure, access-control and security-camera infrastructure, network hardware field deployment, and Division 28 fire-alarm rough-in coordination — with testing, labeling and closeout documentation provided based on project requirements and agreed scope.

Deliver clean, documented low-voltage field execution with clear scope, trade coordination, testing, and closeout.
