What the proposal covers

The proposal identifies the included areas and quantities, cable type, pathway assumptions, termination points, and any labeling, testing and turnover records. A site survey may be needed to verify routes or telecom-room conditions before final pricing.

  • Copper drops: Cat6 or Cat6A runs to work areas, access points and other network devices, with jacks and patch-panel termination as scoped.
  • Fiber and telecom rooms: Single-mode or multimode backbone runs, rack and patch-panel work, or cable management for agreed main or intermediate telecom rooms (MDFs and IDFs).
  • Existing cabling: Add or relocate drops, trace existing runs or clean up rack cabling where included. Identify active circuits and any outage restrictions before work starts.

Testing and turnover

Tell us whether the scope calls for connectivity verification, copper permanent-link certification or fiber acceptance testing. If a specific test is required, include the bid standard, test method and report format. We confirm the appropriate test equipment and field resource before committing to that deliverable.

Handoff can include a drop or port map, matching labels at the jack and patch panel, specified test reports, photos, and marked-up as-built drawings. The proposal lists which records your team will receive. New conduit, electrical work, active network equipment or configuration, extra drops, and additional work outside the agreed access window require separate scope or approval.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you price from plans or an RFQ?

Yes. Send the plans or written scope through the quote request form. Share any assumptions you already know, especially drop counts, pathway responsibility and the required turnover records. We’ll follow up on details that affect pricing or need a site survey.

Can you work in an occupied building?

We scope additions and retrofits in existing commercial space. Tell us which rooms will be occupied, when crews can access them, and whether any active circuits or outage windows need coordination.

Can you scope fiber or rack work?

Yes. Identify the rooms, floors or buildings the fiber connects and specify the fiber type and test method if known. For rack work, send the rack locations and note which patch panels and equipment are part of the request.
