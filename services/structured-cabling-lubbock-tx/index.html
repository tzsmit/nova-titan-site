Scope & Deliverables

Projects are scoped from the available drawings, SOW, and known site conditions. When field conditions need to be verified, we schedule a site survey before final pricing. Proposals define the included installation, testing, and closeout deliverables.

  • Site Survey — When needed, we walk the space, verify pathway and telecom room conditions, and confirm field conditions before final pricing or mobilization.
  • Installation — Clean pulls, labeled runs, proper bend radius, secured pathways.
  • Termination — Patch panels, keystones, and faceplates installed and dressed neatly.
  • Certification — Fluke DSX cable certification with pass/fail documentation.
  • Closeout — As-built documentation, port maps, and labeled infrastructure delivered at completion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you install CAT6 and CAT6A in existing commercial buildings?

Yes. We install CAT6 and CAT6A copper cabling in both new construction and existing commercial buildings, including tenant improvements and retrofits where cabling has to be pulled around an occupied space.

Can Nova Titan quote structured cabling from drawings or an RFQ?

Yes. Send plans, an SOW, or an RFQ package through our quote request form and we will review it and follow up with scope questions or a proposal.

Do you install and terminate fiber optic cabling?

Yes. We install and terminate single-mode and multi-mode fiber optic cabling for backbone runs between floors, buildings, or telecom rooms.

Do you provide cable testing, labeling, and closeout documentation?

Structured cabling closeout can include labeled runs, Fluke DSX certification results, as-built documentation, and port maps based on the project requirements and agreed scope.

Can you build or clean up MDF/IDF and rack infrastructure?

Yes. We build out new MDF/IDF telecom rooms and patch panel/rack infrastructure, and we can clean up or re-terminate existing infrastructure where that work is within scope.
