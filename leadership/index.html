Nova Titan Systems LLC

Project Accountability

Clear scope, coordinated field delivery and documented closeout.

Nova Titan manages commercial low-voltage projects from scope review through customer handoff. Field resources are assigned project by project, with capability, availability and responsibilities confirmed for the work.

Scope and Planning

Drawings, site conditions, deliverables and exclusions are reviewed before a proposal. Changes are documented for approval before additional work proceeds.

Field Coordination

Scheduling is confirmed against the site requirements and available field resources. Required tools, testing and licensed trade responsibilities are established for the approved scope.

Project Communication

Customers receive a project communication route for scheduling, site questions and change decisions. Nova Titan coordinates with the customer and other trades throughout delivery.

Closeout and Handoff

Photos, labeling, test results and closeout documents are provided as required by the approved scope and customer acceptance requirements.
